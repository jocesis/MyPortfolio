About Me

I'm a college student studying Information Technology at Nova Scotia Community College. I enjoy exploring how technology works and developing practical solutions through programming and web development.

Throughout my studies, I've gained experience working with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Java, C#, PHP, SQL, and modern web development tools. I've also worked with technologies such as Next.js, React, ASP.NET, MySQL, Docker, and GitHub.

I enjoy challenging myself with new projects, learning from mistakes, and improving my problem-solving skills. My coursework has allowed me to explore different areas of IT, including application development, database management, server configuration, and deployment.

My goal is to continue growing as an IT professional while developing the technical and communication skills needed to contribute to a professional team.
